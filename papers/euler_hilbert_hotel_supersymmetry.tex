\begin{abstract}
We give two exact operator constructions. The unilateral shift on $\ell^2(\N)$, combined with a spinorial $\mathbb Z_2$ grading, closes to standard $\mathcal N=2$ supersymmetric quantum mechanics; for the $k$-step shift its Witten index equals the Fredholm index, $\Delta_W=\indop(S^k)=-k$. Separately, four regular-tetrahedron null directions generate a simplicial causal cone. Their rank-one two-spinor lifts, after one fermionic CAR mode is attached to each ray, close to the $3+1$ dimensional $\mathcal N=1$ supertranslation algebra. The results are exact in the stated models; physical supersymmetry, a theory of everything, and any implication for open number-theory problems remain open.
\end{abstract}
