\begin{abstract}
We separate two supersymmetry constructions into independently checkable theorem modules. First, every bounded Fredholm operator gives the standard doubled-space $\mathcal N=2$ supersymmetric quantum-mechanical factorization, with Witten index equal to the Fredholm index. Specializing to Toeplitz operators on Hardy space yields
\[
\Delta_W(T_f)=-\operatorname{wind}(f),
\]
so the Hilbert-Hotel shift $S^k=T_{z^k}$ is the monomial case $\Delta_W=-k$. For finite Blaschke products this identifies analytic zero multiplicity with the dimension of the protected supersymmetric zero-mode space. The index is homotopy and compact-perturbation protected.

Second, the regular tetrahedral qubit frame is shown to be exactly the projective Pauli $V_4$ orbit of one fiducial SIC projector. Its determinant-one Pauli lift is $Q_8$, and adjoining the canonical threefold spin rotation gives the binary tetrahedral group $2T$. The tetrahedral null-spinor/CAR construction then realizes the $3+1$ dimensional $\mathcal N=1$ supertranslation algebra and admits a binary-tetrahedral equivariant formulation. Standard component theorems are identified as such; the contribution of this note is the explicit synthesis, modular theorem packaging, and exact crosswalk between the index and tetrahedral-spinor sectors.
\end{abstract}
